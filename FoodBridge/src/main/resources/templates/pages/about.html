About FoodBridge

Every day, good food is thrown away while people nearby go hungry. FoodBridge is a small, practical bridge between the two.

Who it is for

  • Donors: restaurants, hotels, bakeries, supermarkets, event organizers and individuals with surplus food.
  • NGOs and recipients: charities, shelters and community groups.
  • Volunteers: people who collect and deliver donations.
  • Administrators: keep the platform safe and fair.

How we keep it fair

  • Quantity is reserved only when a donor accepts a request, and the system prevents the same food being booked twice.
  • Listings carry pickup deadlines, allergen and storage information, and expire automatically.
  • Full addresses are shared only with the people involved in an accepted request.
Food-safety notice. FoodBridge coordinates donations. It does not inspect, test or certify food. Donors and recipients are responsible for following the food-safety rules that apply to them.
